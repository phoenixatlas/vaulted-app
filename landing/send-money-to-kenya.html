Send money to Kenya.

Pay in pounds or euros; your recipient receives Kenyan shillings (KES). Family support, school fees and emergencies — M-Pesa is how most Kenyans receive money.

How it works

  1. Enter the amount in GBP or EUR and fund by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank transfer.
  2. Vaulted converts to a regulated USD stablecoin and moves it on the cheapest low-cost network.
  3. Our local payout partner pays your recipient in Kenyan shillings (KES).

How your recipient gets paid

  • M-Pesa mobile money, straight to the recipient’s phone
  • Kenyan bank accounts

What you’ll need

  • The recipient’s M-Pesa-registered phone number (2547XXXXXXXX)
  • Their full name as registered with M-Pesa

Planned fees

Free tier: 3 cross-border sends a month at a 1.5% service fee (minimum £0.50, maximum £9.99). Vault Pro (£9.99/month) halves the fee. See pricing.

Corridor status

Sandbox-tested with our payout partner (test money only). Rates and payouts have been tested end-to-end in our partner’s sandbox. Live transfers open corridor by corridor once applicable FCA authorizations are in place — see our launch stages.

Vaulted is not yet FCA-authorized and is not open for customer transactions. Fees and timings are illustrative targets.
