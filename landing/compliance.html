Compliance

Where we are, honestly.

Vaulted is in build. This page sets out our regulatory status, what is and isn’t live, and how we plan to launch.

Regulatory status

Phoenix-Atlas Technologies Ltd is registered in England and Wales (Company No. 17432346). Vaulted is not currently authorized by the Financial Conduct Authority. Cross-border money remittance, cryptoasset custody and cryptoasset exchange services will only be offered upon receipt of applicable authorizations. Nothing on this site is an offer of regulated services or investment advice.

Launch stages

Now — waitlist & preview. A preview app and closed beta use test money only. No customer transactions.
Sandbox corridors. Payout partners are integrated in sandbox / test mode to validate rates and delivery.
Authorization & partner go-live. Applicable UK authorizations and local partner approvals, corridor by corridor.
Phase 2 — reverse settlement. GBP/EUR payouts for the Africa-to-UK/EU corridor via a UK payment-service-provider integration.

Identity checks & data

Live services will require identity verification (KYC) before money moves. We collect only what is needed to provide the service; see our privacy policy. Private keys and encrypted messages are never accessible to us (security model).

Risk warning

Don’t invest unless you’re prepared to lose all the money you invest. This is a high-risk investment and you should not expect to be protected if something goes wrong. Read the full risk disclosure.
