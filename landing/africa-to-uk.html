How the reverse rail works

  1. Fund locally — bank transfer in Nigeria and South Africa, M-Pesa in Kenya, MoMo in Ghana.
  2. Funds convert to a regulated USD stablecoin (USDC) and move on a low-cost network.
  3. The UK or EU recipient is paid in GBP (Faster Payments) or EUR (SEPA Instant).

GBP and EUR settlement requires our UK payment-service-provider integration (Phase 2). Today the reverse corridor offers rate quotes and a waitlist. Learn more in stablecoins vs bank transfers.
