How to send money from the UK to Nigeria and Kenya

The main ways to send money from the UK to Nigeria and Kenya, what to compare (fees, exchange rate, speed, payout method) and the details your recipient needs.

Your main options

  • Bank wire (SWIFT). Widely available but often the slowest and most expensive route, with fees taken by intermediary banks and a marked-up exchange rate.
  • Money-transfer apps. Specialist providers usually beat banks on price and speed; costs vary by corridor and payout method.
  • Mobile money. In Kenya, M-Pesa is the most common way to receive money — funds land on the recipient’s phone.
  • Stablecoin rails. Newer services convert pounds to a dollar stablecoin, move it on a blockchain network and pay out local currency. This is the approach Vaulted is building.

What to compare before you send

  1. Total cost — the fee and the exchange-rate margin. Compare what the recipient actually receives.
  2. Speed — seconds, minutes or days.
  3. Payout method — bank account, mobile money or cash pickup.
  4. Regulation — check the provider is authorized to offer money transfers in the UK.

Details your recipient needs to give you

Nigeria

  • Their 10-digit bank account number (NUBAN) and bank name.
  • Their full name exactly as on the account — good providers confirm it before you pay.

Kenya

  • Their M-Pesa-registered phone number (format 2547XXXXXXXX), or bank details.
  • Their full name as registered with M-Pesa.

Stay safe

  • Only send to people you know. Scammers often pose as relatives or romantic partners.
  • Double-check numbers — transfers are hard to reverse.
  • Keep the receipt and reference.
