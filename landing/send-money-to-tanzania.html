Send money to Tanzania.

Pay in pounds or euros; your recipient receives Tanzanian shillings (TZS). Support for family and small businesses via mobile money.

How it works

  1. Enter the amount in GBP or EUR and fund by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank transfer.
  2. Vaulted converts to a regulated USD stablecoin and moves it on the cheapest low-cost network.
  3. Our local payout partner pays your recipient in Tanzanian shillings (TZS).

How your recipient gets paid

  • Mobile-money wallets

What you’ll need

  • The recipient’s mobile-money number
  • Their full name as registered

Planned fees

Free tier: 3 cross-border sends a month at a 1.5% service fee (minimum £0.50, maximum £9.99). Vault Pro (£9.99/month) halves the fee. See pricing.

Corridor status

Supported in our payout partner’s sandbox. Our payout partner supports this destination in test mode; end-to-end tests are next. Live transfers open corridor by corridor once applicable FCA authorizations are in place — see our launch stages.

Vaulted is not yet FCA-authorized and is not open for customer transactions. Fees and timings are illustrative targets.
