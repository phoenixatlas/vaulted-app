How it will work

Three steps to sovereignty.

The flow below describes Vaulted’s planned end-to-end experience once we’re live. New to crypto? You never have to touch it — fund with a card, Apple Pay or bank transfer, and we’ll do the rest. Crypto-native? Send USDC, XLM, XRP or SOL from any wallet and skip the fiat rails entirely.

01
Create your vault

Sign up in under a minute. We generate a 12-word recovery phrase on your device — write it down somewhere safe. It never touches our servers.

02
Fund your transfer — your choice

New to crypto? Top up with a debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or a UK bank transfer — pounds in, pounds available. Crypto-native? Send USDC, BTC, ETH, SOL, XLM or XRP straight to your Vaulted address (one 0x covers every EVM chain).

03
Send money in seconds

Pick a country → enter pounds → confirm. Vaulted will auto-pick the cheapest chain and settle in ~5–30 seconds. Recipient gets local currency to M-Pesa, mobile-money or bank. Target fees from £0.50. Live once authorized.
